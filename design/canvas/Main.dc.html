Principles, from the evidence

01 · Issue 3

Acknowledge instantly

Every submission answers within a second: “Received, we’re on it.” Silence is the top complaint, not the repair.

02 · Issue 3, 4

Always show the stage

The four-stage tracker appears wherever a ticket does: logged, booked, in progress, resolved.

03 · Issue 6

Ask, so they don’t have to complain

The system asks first (“Is your room cold?”). One tap to confirm; one photo to report. No forms.

04 · Product §9

Problem-first for landlords

Issue, urgency and due time lead every row. Names and phone numbers live one level down.

05 · Issue 12

Urgency is never colour alone

Each level pairs a label, an icon shape and a hue of distinct lightness. Overdue is stated in words.

06 · Issue 5

One problem, one ticket

Building-wide issues get their own surface (the status bar) so neighbours see it’s known before they report it.
